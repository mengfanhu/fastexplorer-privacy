Fast Explorer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Version: 0.1.0.0

Fast Explorer (the "App") is a local file management and search tool. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information. By using the App, you understand this policy.

1. Information We Collect and Use

The App's core features — file browsing, tag management, file indexing, full-text search, OCR recognition, and document preview — all run entirely on your device. The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask for identity information such as your name, phone number, or address.

  • Local data: Your settings, tag library, indexes, and cache are stored locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FasterFinder and are not automatically uploaded to our servers. We do not operate or access this local data.
  • Search terms: If you use the web search feature (when enabled), the keywords you enter are sent to the relevant third-party search engine (such as DuckDuckGo) to return results. Such requests are governed by that third party's privacy policy. Local file searches do not use the network.
  • Activation and purchases: Store edition subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Microsoft Store. We do not collect your payment information through the App.

2. Information Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. Data may be shared only in the following cases:

  • When you use web search, your keywords are sent to the corresponding search engine provider;
  • When you actively export tags or use the "shared tags" feature, tag information is written, as directed by you, to a file or file metadata you choose. This is entirely under your control and does not pass through us.

3. Data Storage and Security

All local data is stored only on your device and is protected by your device's account and file-system permissions. The App contains no functionality to upload user files, tags, or indexes to any remote server.

4. Your Controls

  • You can manage, export, or delete tags and index data at any time through Settings;
  • Uninstalling the App removes the program files. To fully remove data, delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\FasterFinder folder;
  • If you do not wish to use web search, you can leave it disabled; local features are unaffected.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Policy Updates

When we add a feature that affects privacy, we will update this policy and publish the revision date here.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us by email: franklynmeng@gmail.com.
